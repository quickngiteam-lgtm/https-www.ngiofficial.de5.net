About NGI

About Next Generation Initiative

An independent, student-led youth initiative committed to driving constructive social transformation and grassroots community action.

Our Background

Based in Mirpurkhas, Sindh, NGI unites rural and urban youth to tackle local challenges through education, awareness, and community-driven initiatives.

Core Objectives

Promoting youth leadership, civic responsibility, and equal opportunities for personal and social development across Pakistan.

How We Work

Next Generation Initiative (NGI) is an independent, student-led youth initiative. NGI works in collaboration with non-governmental organizations (NGOs) legally registered with the relevant authorities in Pakistan to implement flagship programmes and partnership-based projects. Through these collaborations, NGI mobilizes young people and supports community-led initiatives in education, awareness, leadership development and social transformation. NGI operates independently as a youth-led initiative; these partnerships do not imply that NGI itself is a registered NGO.
