Next Generation Initiative (NGI)

Next Generation Initiative (NGI) is an independent, student-led youth initiative based in Mirpurkhas, Sindh, dedicated to uniting rural and urban youth and driving constructive social transformation across Pakistan.

NGI collaborates with non-governmental organizations (NGOs) legally registered with the relevant authorities in Pakistan to implement flagship programmes and partnership-based projects. Through these collaborations, we mobilize young people and advance education, civic awareness, leadership development, and grassroots community action. NGI operates independently as a youth-led initiative; these partnerships do not imply that NGI itself is a registered NGO.

Education & Equal Opportunity

Educate a Girl. Build a Brighter Future.

Every girl in Pakistan deserves the opportunity to learn, grow, and fulfil her potential. Yet many girls continue to face barriers to education. We must work together to remove these barriers and ensure that every girl has access to safe, inclusive, and quality learning opportunities.

Girls are the architects of tomorrow. When we invest in their education, we empower future leaders, innovators, and changemakers who will strengthen their communities and contribute to a more prosperous, equitable Pakistan. At NGI, we believe that educating girls is not only a fundamental right, but a powerful foundation for a better future for all.
